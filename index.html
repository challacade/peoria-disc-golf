How this guide works

Difficulty, explained

PDGA doesn't publish a difficulty rating, so ours is an estimate based on course length, hole distances, terrain, foliage and course descriptions.

    Where the data comes from

    • PDGA Course Directory: holes, length, year established, targets, tees, amenities and directions.
    • OpenStreetMap: course locations and some posted hours.
    • Park district and operator websites: hours, admission and day-pass prices.
    • Open-Meteo: live weather, fetched in your browser.
    • Sunrise and sunset are calculated on your device with NOAA's solar equations.

    Course details were last checked . Hours marked unverified are typical park hours, so confirm with the operator before you go.

    Play it right

    • Private means private. Church, camp and club courses need permission or a fee.
    • Check the gate. Some parks close their gates in winter or during events.
    • Carry in, carry out. Pack out your trash and let faster groups play through.
    • Mark your discs. Write a phone number on the rim. Ravines and creeks eat plastic.
